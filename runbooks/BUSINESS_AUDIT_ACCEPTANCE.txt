Business audit fixes — 2026-10-25

Deployment
----------
Apply embedded migration 00012 with backend/cmd/migrate before starting the
updated API and worker. Deploy the matching frontend/API contract together.
The migration repairs only automatic "other" owner-loan categories, retains
user corrections, queues affected companies and resolves paid/cancelled overdue
alerts. No ML source, weights, training or prediction paths are changed.

Product rules
-------------
Statements older than seven days, future-dated statements, fallback/low-confidence
forecasts and unconfirmed active recurring schedules cannot supply a spending
recommendation. safe_to_spend=0 is retained for older API consumers; consult
spending_available and spending_restrictions before displaying an allowance.
The UI labels the actual statement date and the final forecast date explicitly.
All risk screens respect probability_reliable. A stochastic risk with a positive
median balance displays shortfall_p90 as an adverse-case amount, rather than zero.

Invoices may explicitly replace one unpaid manual or confirmed detected recurring
event through replaces_event_id. Company ownership, direction, counterparty and
single use are validated under the company lock. The original remains linked
provenance. Removing an unpaid invoice restores the original plan. Paid history
and bank-settlement reservations survive removing the invoice.

Missing plan values and deviations are null, not zero. A saved model plan can
only be compared with dates after its actual creation. Explicit document plans
retain their full amount through partial/full payment and are labelled separately.
Resolved overdue alerts are retained in notification history but excluded from
the dashboard. A recurring overdue condition reopens its existing notification.

New confirmed tax entries include their full schedule by default. The UI offers
an explicit calendar-only alternative. Existing liabilities are not reclassified
automatically. Ordinary ENP/USN/VAT double-entry checks remain enforced.

Marketplace accounting rows remain separate from bank facts. The owner can
confirm an expected payout amount/date/payer and create a manual forecast event
with visible report provenance. request_id prevents a repeat submission from
creating a second event; conflicting retries return 409. A bank statement is
still required to establish actual cash.

allowlisting and read credentials. Synthetic acceptance is not a live pass.
